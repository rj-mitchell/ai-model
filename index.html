The equations

1 · Sector level: net real economic value

\[\epsilon_i(T)=(1-f_i)(1-g_i(T)) \qquad S_i(T)=\frac{1}{\epsilon_i(T)+\dfrac{1-\epsilon_i(T)}{T_{\text{eff}}}}\]
\[V_i(T)=\frac{1+p_i\big(S_i(T)-1\big)}{1+\big(\eta_i\ln T_{\text{eff}}+\gamma_i\ln T\big)\,S_i(T)}\]

Deployment rule. Private workflows use \(T_{\text{eff}}=\min(T,T^*)\): firms stop scaling usage at their Slop Peak \(T^*\), found here as the usage level that maximises \(V_i\). Open-intake commons use \(T_{\text{eff}}=T\), because senders push verification costs onto receivers.

2 · Macro level: two-tier aggregation

\[V_{\text{core}}=\Big(\sum_{i\in\text{Core}}\tilde w_i V_i^{\rho}\Big)^{1/\rho} \qquad V_{\text{flex}}=\sum_{j\in\text{Flex}}\tilde w_j V_j\]
\[V_{\text{macro}}(T)=V_{\text{core}}^{\,W_{\text{core}}}\,\big(W_{\text{core}}V_{\text{core}}+W_{\text{flex}}V_{\text{flex}}\big)^{W_{\text{flex}}}\]

At baseline (\(T=1\), \(g_i=0\)) every sector and the macro economy equal exactly 1.0.

3 · Dynamic loop: tech power

\[T_{t+1}=T_t\left[1+\frac{r_0\,\big(V_{\text{R\&D},t}\big)^{\phi}\,\min(1,V_{\text{core},t})}{1+T_t/T_{\text{half}}}\right]\]

Baseline progress \(r_0\), boosted by the AI R&D sector's own value (recursive self-improvement, strength \(\phi\)) and braked when Core infrastructure falls below baseline. Diminishing returns halve growth at \(T_{\text{half}}\) and keep slowing it beyond, without ever stopping it. Starts from \(T_0=1\) at end-2026.

4 · Dynamic loop: S-curve redesign

\[g_i^*(T_t)=\frac{g_{i,\max}}{1+\big(T_{\text{crit},i}/T_t\big)^{k}} \qquad g_{i,t+1}=g_{i,t}+\alpha_i\big(g_i^*(T_t)-g_{i,t}\big)\]

Each sector has a smooth target level of redesign that rises with \(T\) and reaches half its ceiling at \(T_{\text{crit},i}\) (steepness \(k=2.5\)). Actual redesign chases that target with institutional lag \(\alpha_i\), starting from \(g_{i,0}=0\). Early adopters move slowly, adoption is fastest around \(T_{\text{crit},i}\), then it rounds off toward \(g_{i,\max}\).

T
Useful AI work: verified work per £ on the compressible slice of a job, as a multiple of end-2026 (1×). Not raw tokens.
fi
Native fit: share of the existing workflow AI compresses out of the box.
gi
Gustafson redesign: share of the remaining bottleneck removed by re-architecting the workflow (capped at 90%).
pi
Real wealth share: 1 means the speedup creates wealth, 0 means a zero-sum Red Queen arms race.
ηi
Internal slop tax: review and verification drag from the sector's own AI use (slider 0–10 maps to 0–0.5).
γi
Ambient slop exposure: external spam, fraud and synthetic load (slider 0–10 maps to 0–0.5; 0 if air-gapped).
ρ
Core complementarity: ρ = 1 − fragility. ρ = −4 is the Jones–Tonetti weak-link case.
w̃, W
Sector weights within each tier (normalised), and the Core / Flex split of the economy.
